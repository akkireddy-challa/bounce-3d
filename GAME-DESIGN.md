## 🌟 1. High Concept & Commercial Market Niche

*Bounce 3D* is a modern 3D physics reimagining of the iconic 2000s Nokia mobile title *Bounce*.

In the global mobile games market, physics-based rolling ball games represent one of the most lucrative and evergreen genres in history (*Going Balls*, *Rolling Sky*, *Ballance*). Their commercial success stems from three factors:
1. **Immediate Tactile Feedback**: Physics-driven momentum, angular friction, and bouncy restitution feel naturally satisfying without requiring tutorials.
2. **Effortless Mobile Controls**: Virtual analog touch thumbsticks translate 1:1 to spherical movement.
3. **High Replayability & Speedrunning**: The desire to shave 0.5 seconds off a run and collect every floating ring drives viral social competition.

## 🕹️ 2. Core Gameplay Mechanics

### The Player (The Red Bouncy Ball)
- **Controls**:
  - `WASD` / Arrow Keys: Roll forward/back/left/right with realistic ball momentum and rotation.
  - `Spacebar` / On-Screen Jump Button: Bounce off the ground.
  - Camera: Smooth third-person follow cam that dynamically aligns with the ball's velocity vector.

### Classic Nokia Bounce Elements in 3D:
1. **The Golden Hoops (Objective)**:
   - Glowing golden rings hovering in the air.
   - The player must roll or bounce *through* the center of the ring.
   - Ring plays a classic satisfying "Chime!" sound and lights up green once cleared.
   - The Exit Portal only opens once all rings are collected.
2. **Yellow Rubber Trampolines (Super Bounce)**:
   - Stepping on a yellow bouncy pad propels the ball 4x higher into the air to reach elevated platforms and high rings.
3. **The Red Spikes (Hazard)**:
   - Geometric triangular red spikes. Touching a spike pops the ball with a cartoon "POP!" sound and triggers a 0.6s checkpoint respawn.
4. **The Air Pump (Size Morphing Power-Up)**:
   - **Inflate Power-Up**: Ball expands to 2x size, becomes lightweight, bounces higher, and floats on water/energy streams.
   - **Deflate Power-Up**: Ball shrinks to mini size, moving faster and fitting through tight pipes and narrow crevices.
5. **The Exit Portal (Stage Complete)**:
   - Swirling green portal gate that unlocks when all hoops are collected.

## 🎨 3. Art Direction & Visual Aesthetics

- **The Ball**: High-gloss candy-apple red (`#EF4444`) with shiny white specular reflection highlight and subtle squash-and-stretch on bounce.
- **The World**:
  - Checkerboard floating pathways in sky blue (`#38BDF8`) and cloud white (`#FFFFFF`).
  - Warm golden rings (`#FBBF24`) emitting gentle yellow particle dust.
  - Bright yellow trampoline pads with animated spring compressions.
  - Menacing ruby red spikes (`#DC2626`).
  - Background: Deep vivid blue skybox with floating low-poly cumulus clouds.

## 📱 4. Mobile & Desktop Parity
- **Desktop**: WASD or Arrow Keys for torque application, Spacebar for jump impulses, Mouse for camera orbit.
- **Mobile**: Dynamic floating virtual thumbstick on the left screen hemisphere, dedicated tactile jump button on the right thumb, with automatic smooth follow-camera tracking.
